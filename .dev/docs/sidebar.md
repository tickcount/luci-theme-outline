# Sidebar & menu

- `header.ut`: near-minimal shell — empty `#sidebar` plus a parser-blocking inline script right after it that replays the sidebar cache (below) before first paint; sidebar chrome + nav are otherwise built client-side in `menu-outline.js`.
- `sidebar-outline.js`: state machine for theme (light/dark/device), sidebar collapse/expand, accordion, and mobile drawer — exposed as `window.OutlineSidebar` after the `outline-sidebar-ready` event fires.
- `menu-outline.js`: builds the sidebar chrome after ChatGPT's sidebar — a 52px icon **rail** (logo/home, one button per top-level section, logout at the foot) beside a 238px **panel** (title row: hostname, the command palette's search icon `#cmdk-trigger` bound by `initPalette`, the collapse toggle; then the nav). It resolves the `admin` branch of `ui.menu.load()` into one `.sidebar-accordion-item[data-section]` group per section; the panel shows **one section at a time** (`.is-shown` on the group and its rail button) — the current page's on load, any other picked from the rail without navigating (`sidebar-outline.js`). Collapsed, only the rail stays: a rail button navigates to its section and hovering it lists the section's pages. `ICON_MAP` maps a LuCI menu node's `name` to `/outline/icons/*.svg`; a section it doesn't know (an app's own top-level node — LuCI's menu schema drops any `icon` key, so the name is all the theme gets) gets a monogram of its title instead, a lucide-style data-SVG `<img>` so the `img.outline-icon` rules still apply; deeper levels render as `#tabmenu`. `syncRoute()` re-marks the active page, rail button and shown section (same longest-link-prefix rule as the header.ut replay), breadcrumb and tabs from `L.env` after a [router](router.md) swap without rebuilding the sidebar; `closeSurfaces()` closes palette/drawer/popover.

## Sidebar cache (anti-flash)

`menu-outline.js` snapshots `#sidebar.innerHTML` + scroll position into `sessionStorage['outline.sidebar.cache']` (`{v, lang, html, scroll}`) after render and on `pagehide`. The `header.ut` inline script replays it pre-paint on the next navigation, recomputes the active highlight for the current URL (longest link-path prefix — keep in sync with menu-outline's dispatchpath matching), restores accordion/scroll state, and sets `data-outline-built` / `data-outline-restored` on `#sidebar`.

When restored, `renderSidebarChrome` only re-syncs the hostname, and `renderSidebarNav` preserves accordion/scroll across its authoritative rebuild.

Restored HTML loses inline JS handlers (`innerHTML` serialization), so anything that must work before the re-render needs a delegated listener — e.g. the logout click in `header.ut`, which clears the cache and sets `window.outlineSuppressSidebarCache` so the `pagehide` re-cache stays suppressed.

**Bump `v` whenever the sidebar markup changes shape** (now 8: section rows as buttons with an icon and a chevron, the brand logo, the panel's foot logout and the rail's search). The replay also re-folds the sections: the current one open, others only if the visitor opened them (`data-user`).

## Navigation styles (Appearance › Navigation)

`html[data-nav]` is the kind (`sidebar` | `navbar`), `html[data-nav-style]` the layout; header.ut sets both from `outline.appearance` (`navigation`, `nav_style`). The styles are desktop-only rules in `_nav.css`, after Synara's sidebar; phones keep the drawer under the floating bar whatever the style. The tones are ink alphas from the palette (`--foreground-aNN`, listed in `tokens/spec.js` `ALPHAS` and written by `palette.derive()`) — the build forbids token-based `color-mix()`, so opaque grounds are the page colour with a tint gradient over it.

- **Sidebars** (`docked`, `floating`, `inset`): one 256px panel — brand row (logo, name, search, collapse), every section a folding row (`button.sidebar-group-label`: icon, name, chevron; `data-open` on its group, toggled by a delegated click in `sidebar-outline.js`), the current one open, Log out at the foot (`.sidebar-panel-foot`). Rows are 28px, 8px radius, 2px apart. Collapsed (its button or ⌘B / Ctrl+B) the 52px rail stays: logo (hover → expand), search (`.sidebar-rail-search`, delegated to the palette), sections, logout; hovering a section lists its pages 6px past the sidebar. Docked takes a shade off the page with a hairline seam; floating is a 12px card with a ring and a shadow (in dark, a lighter tone); inset puts the page in a 12px card 3px off the window, the sidebar and top bar on the ground.
- **Navbars** (`pills`, `bar`): the shared `html[data-nav="navbar"]` block in `_layout.css` (tooltips off, the panel dissolved, the floating top bar on phones and its scroll blur), then per style. **Pills** is the floating pill row, the sections in Synara's capsule (ink-tint track, raised thumb). **Bar** is one 48px row across the top under a hairline: brand, the sections as text buttons (the current one filled), the indicators, theme, search and logout on the right. In both a section's pages drop under it on hover or click (the collapsed rail's popover; its trigger gets `.is-open`).

## Navbar layout (pills)

Appearance › Navigation › Navbar — the default; only an explicit `sidebar` opts out — sets `html[data-nav="navbar"]` (header.ut, from `outline.appearance.navigation`), Pills its default style. It is the same markup laid out by the `Navbar layout` blocks at the end of `_layout.css` (shared, then `html[data-nav-style="pills"]`), desktop only: the rail home becomes a brand pill (logo + hostname label), the rail nav a centred section pill (icon + label), the panel is dropped (`display: contents`, nav hidden) except the ⌘K button, which joins logout and the top bar's indicators as pills on the right (there is no light/dark switch in the top bar: the mode lives in Appearance and the palette). A section's pages drop under its pill on hover or click — `sidebar-outline.js` reuses the collapsed rail's popover (`_pops()` / `_navbar()`). A navbar has no collapsed state: header.ut's pre-paint script and `sidebar-outline.js` (which now reads `html[data-sidebar-collapsed]`) both treat it as expanded. Both layouts keep a drawer on phones -- the sidebar's panel alone (no rail, no collapse button; every section folds, rows 36px), on a dense frost over a backdrop -- under a top bar that follows the chosen navbar: Bar's opaque row with flat buttons (`_nav.css`, clear over a backdrop and frosted once scrolled), otherwise the floating pills without the section pill: header.ut's `.topbar-brand` (logo + hostname; hidden in every other case) on the left, the indicators and the drawer's burger on the right — `.card-header-left` turns `display: contents` so its pieces order with the right group, and the breadcrumb is hidden. The bar sits at `z-[25]`: over the scroll blur, under the drawer's overlay. The header's icon filter is scoped to `.card-header button img`, so the brand logo keeps its colours. The drawer's slide (`#sidebar[data-drawer-open]`) only runs once the burger has been used: crossing the breakpoint either way resets the drawer (closed, attribute removed, page scrollable), so a window narrowed past it doesn't slide the sidebar out across the page.

The side pills line up with the content column's edges (`--nav-edge`). When the row is wider than the column, the edge gives way: `sidebar-outline.js` keeps `--nav-reserve` (half the section pill + the wider side group + an 8px gap) up to date with a `ResizeObserver`, and `--nav-edge` takes the smaller of the column edge and `50% - --nav-reserve`, down to 12px.

The theme captures no view transitions (`components/_view-transitions.css` sets `:root { view-transition-name: none }`); swapped and freshly loaded content fades in instead. On desktop, collapsing eases the rail's width (`_layout.css`) and `#main-wrapper`, a flex-1 sibling, follows it frame by frame: the content is pushed, never snapped and slid back. `sidebar-outline.js` sets `html[data-sidebar-anim]` on the first toggle so a page load never plays the transition.
